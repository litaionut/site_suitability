Import TI Bins from CSV

CSV Format

The CSV file must be UTF-8 encoded with a header row containing the following columns:

  • v_center_mps (required): Bin center wind speed (m/s)
  • hours (required): Hours in this bin
  • mean_sigma_mps (required): Mean standard deviation (m/s)
  • std_sigma_mps (optional): Standard deviation of sigma (m/s). May be empty (COV=0.3 will be flagged during assessment)
